Our Story

A little bit of luxury, right here in Nalhati.

Food Villa brought something new to town: a clean, bright, spacious restaurant where families, friends and couples can sit back and enjoy a proper meal. Our chefs cook everything to order, from charcoal-kissed kababs and rich mutton kosha to Hakka noodles, pizzas and sizzlers.

We're proud of our food, and just as proud of our team. Guests often mention our staff by name.
